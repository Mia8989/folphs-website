Together we can achieve

The 2026-27 goals

Since CPS funding is always in flux, gifts to FOLPHS make a real difference in the LPHS budget. The Chicago Tribune reported this summer on how Chicago schools are turning to outside revenue amid the CPS budget crunch.

  1. Update exercise equipment

    Support student stamina and wellness with new fitness equipment.

  2. Keep one Chromebook for every student

    Replace aging laptops to maintain the 1:1 Chromebook ratio.

  3. Fund student scholarships

    Help students take on academic endeavors and national competitions.

  4. Protect music and drama

    Weather-safe storage for music and drama department instruments and items.

  5. Build a space for social workers

    A deserving space where social workers can counsel students.

Last year’s results

Over $150,000 raised for LPHS

Thanks to our community, last year’s fall fundraiser reflected LP Lions loyalty in every grade and at every gift size. Together we:

  • Fully funded a $50,000 computer lab with 31 computers, 2 printers and 9 kits
  • Fully funded the history department’s training at the AHA conference
  • Amplified the auditorium with a new speaker
  • Supported sports with gym upgrades, including a new logo and paint
  • Refurbished instruments and paved the way for clubs like Model UN to compete

A letter to our neighbors

Why we’re asking

Dear Future Sponsor, I am personally asking you to invest in us.

Lincoln Park High School, ranked in the top 3% by US News’ list of Best U.S. High Schools, is the hub for academic consistency, creativity and community! We represent nearly 2,200 students from all city zip codes; 93% are college-bound, 86%+ enroll in AP courses, plus plenty of participation in over 60 clubs and more than 25 sports.

Thanks to our community, last year’s fall fundraiser was a success: we raised about $150,000 to build a new computer lab (equipped with 3-D printers), amplify our auditorium with a new speaker, and send teachers to training. But there is so much more to do, now!

As a local, neighborhood business, your financial support is not only a chance to assist us, but is also a unique opportunity to gain goodwill with widespread recognition, both within and around our school community; we’ll provide prime advertising on Armitage St. as well as social media recognition, so that together, we can achieve all these goals.

Since CPS is always in flux, your investment equals 6% of LPHS’ budget, and is essential to enriching our experiences! (See: Chicago Tribune.)

Deepen your connection to our community, demonstrate commitment to education, and know your neighborhood patrons. We hope you’ll help.

In partnership,

Roona Shah

Mom, Parent, and Secretary for Friends of Lincoln Park High School

A parent-run, volunteer organization supporting every student at Lincoln Park High School in Chicago.
